Screen Time access

Mujo needs your permission to use Apple's Screen Time technologies. You can review or revoke this permission through Apple settings.

Usage estimates

Mujo receives activity updates from the operating system. Estimates can update with a delay and may not exactly match the value currently shown in Apple's Screen Time settings.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. You can change Mujo's notification settings in the app or in the system Settings app.

Removing local data

Mujo does not have user accounts or server-side app data. Removing the app from your device removes data stored by Mujo and its extensions.

Reporting a problem

Include your Mujo version, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, private Screen Time details, or other sensitive personal information.
